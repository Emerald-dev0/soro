# Brag Plan: Soro — the ₦500 phone call

## What is this app?
Soro is a conversational banking interface: customers speak naturally to Ayo, and Soro's backend turns the conversation into real, authorized, audited financial operations.

## The angle
Not a launch video — a captured banking call. One customer sentence becomes a visible financial workflow. The viewer watches money move because someone talked.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Near-black screen. One line, held: "Banking shouldn't require you to know how to bank." Then a phone rings into the call UI. The question earns the next 22 seconds.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The customer says "Ayo, abeg buy me five hundred naira data" — no menus, no codes — and INTENT: BUY_DATA lands in the Command Center.
- The masked keypad: four dots, no digits, with the line "Ayo never sees your PIN."
- The balance counts ₦84,250 → ₦83,750, then the customer asks "How much dey my account now?" and hears the new balance — proof the state really changed.

## Outro / punchline
Full event timeline in one frame, caption "Simple for the customer. Serious underneath.", then "AI understands. The backend decides." over the Soro wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Speak ("buy me ₦500 data") → confirm ("Yes, go ahead") + authorize (masked PIN) → result (balance changed, re-queried). Entry → key action → result, all inside one call.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: live banking interaction, captured — not advertised
- Interpretation: restraint everywhere; long holds on money moments; motion serves the transaction, never decorates it.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #fafaf8 (off-white), dark scenes #101312
- Accent: #1f4fd6 (restrained blue)
- Text: #131513
- Display font: Inter (800, tight tracking)
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the split call UI + Command Center event timeline; Ayo character poses (welcome, listening, speaking, security, success) from apps/web/public/ayo/

## Share copy (draft)
He just spoke normally — and the banking operation actually happened. Soro: banking shouldn't require you to know how to bank.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed under dialogue; duck under every voice line
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (bundled, 120 BPM)
- Music treatment: low bed from 0s, subtle lift at transaction success (~17.5s), fade out from 23s
- Music cue guidance: preset read (vol-1); strong cues at 17.02s (TRANSACTION SUCCESS), 20.02s (balance proof), 23.02s (outro). Beat grid ~0.5s for sequential event arrivals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; event rows may pulse softly on arrival
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched — call-connect blip, keypad taps (muted), success chime at transaction
- Audio-coupled moments: transcript lines appear as they are spoken; balance counts with soft ticks; event rows arrive one by one on the beat grid
- Restraint rule: dialogue always intelligible; no cinematic booms, no EDM, no sci-fi effects

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Problem — 2.5s
Near-black screen. "Banking shouldn't require you to know how to bank." holds, then a phone-call shimmer wipes into Scene 2.
Sequential/interaction: none (single held line).
Audio intent: music bed starts low; a soft call-connect blip at the wipe.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: low warm bed.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The call starts — 3.5s
Split screen: left phone UI (Ayo welcome pose, "Connected · 00:00", waveform), right Command Center with CALL STARTED + CUSTOMER IDENTIFIED rows arriving. Ayo (voice): "Hi Daniel, how can I help you today?"
Sequential/interaction: yes — event rows arrive one by one; transcript line appears as spoken.
Audio intent: establish the two voices (customer voice, Ayo voice).
Audio-coupled idea: transcript typed/appears in sync with speech.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The request — 3.5s
Customer (voice): "Ayo, abeg buy me five hundred naira data." Transcript emphasized large: "Buy me ₦500 data." Right side: INTENT DETECTED → BUY_DATA slams in.
Sequential/interaction: yes — words emphasize, then intent row arrives.
Audio intent: the pivotal line; music dips under it.
Audio-coupled idea: intent row lands on a beat.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Understanding — 3.5s
Plan card builds: MTN · 1.5GB · 7 days · ₦500 · Recommended. Ayo (voice): "I found a 1.5GB plan valid for 7 days. Want me to get that one?" Right side: DATA PLAN LOOKUP → PLAN RECOMMENDED.
Sequential/interaction: yes — plan card fields arrive one by one.
Audio intent: steady, explanatory.
Audio-coupled idea: card fields arrive on beat grid.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Confirm + authorize — 4.5s
Customer (voice): "Yes, go ahead." CONFIRMATION RECEIVED → AUTHORIZATION REQUIRED. Masked keypad, four dots fill with soft taps. AUTHORIZATION SUCCESS. Caption: "Ayo never sees your PIN."
Sequential/interaction: yes — dots fill one by one with taps.
Audio intent: tension then release; taps then a soft confirm tone.
Audio-coupled idea: dot fills matched to tap sounds.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Money moves + proof — 3.5s
TRANSACTION SUCCESS. Balance counts ₦84,250 → ₦83,750 with soft ticks. Customer (voice): "How much dey my account now?" Ayo (voice): "₦83,750." BALANCE VIEWED lands. Hold the proof beat.
Sequential/interaction: yes — count-up, then the re-query exchange.
Audio intent: the payoff; small swell under the new balance.
Audio-coupled idea: count-up ticks.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Reveal + outro — 4s
Full timeline in one frame (CALL → … → BALANCE UPDATED). Caption: "Simple for the customer. Serious underneath." Then Soro wordmark + "AI understands. The backend decides." + tagline.
Sequential/interaction: timeline rows cascade quickly, then hold.
Audio intent: resolve; music fades from 23s.
Audio-coupled idea: cascade on beat grid, then stillness for the final lines.
Transition mood: soft → end
